## Liquids

Liquid colours: black, dark green, red, transparent red.

Objects that hold liquid:
- scene2_1: transparent_measuring_cylinder_1 (black), transparent_measuring_cylinder_2
  (dark green), each 50% full.
- scene2_2 and scene5: transparent_measuring_cylinder_1 (black), _2 (dark green), _3 (red),
  each 50% full.
- scene8: transparent_test_tube_1, _2 and _3 (transparent red), each 67% full.

## Things to note

- In scene8 the three test tubes stand in grey_test_tube_holder: the same x (0.427) and
  y values within the holder's span. The scene files do not say so, so in the mock world
  their `on` starts as `None`.
- The only caps are the test tube caps of scene8, and there are no lids.
- Every object that holds liquid is graspable, and every container holding liquid starts
  open.
